# Code of Conduct

This project adopts the [Contributor Covenant](https://www.contributor-covenant.org/version/2/1/code_of_conduct/), version 2.1, as its code of conduct.

We are committed to a welcoming, respectful, and harassment-free experience for everyone who participates, regardless of background or identity.

Expected behavior: be considerate, assume good faith, give and accept constructive feedback gracefully, and focus on what is best for the community.

Unacceptable behavior: personal attacks, demeaning comments, and any conduct that a reasonable person would consider inappropriate in a professional setting.

## Reporting

Report concerns privately to **eknuth@gmail.com**. Reports are reviewed and handled confidentially. Maintainers may remove contributions or restrict participation for behavior that violates this code.

The full text, including enforcement guidelines, is available at the Contributor Covenant link above.
